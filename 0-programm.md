The ASE Workshop 2027 will take place on 22 February 2027 at [Nordcampus of TU Dortmund](https://se2027.cs.tu-dortmund.de/veranstaltungsort/).

The programme will include a mix of regular, peer-reviewed papers and invited talks. The workshop will start with a keynote or a panel discussion.

The workshop will end with the annual meeting of the GI specialist group “Automotive Software Engineering.” All workshop participants are also invited to participate in the meeting.
