The workshop will discuss current challenges and new approaches in automotive software engineering. The focus will be on software-defined vehicles (SDV), generative and embedded AI, continuous software updates, and new approaches to end-to-end assurance of safety, security and compliance. In addition to mature research findings, visionary contributions, case studies, and early-stage work are also expressly welcome.

Examples of topics include, but are not limited to:

- Software engineering for software-defined vehicles (SDVs)
- Engineering and assurance of AI-based automotive systems
- Continuous integration, deployment, and updating of vehicle software  
- Continuous evolution of connected vehicle software across the lifecycle
- Data-driven development and data management in automotive software engineering
- Generative AI for automotive software development, verification, and validation
- Over-the-air (OTA) updates: engineering, deployment, and risk management
- Continuous safety assurance and safety cases for evolving systems
- Security engineering and cybersecurity management for connected vehicles
- Privacy-by-design and data protection in vehicle software and services
- Regulatory compliance, homologation, and continuous type approval
- Traceable evidence generation across safety, security, privacy, and compliance
- Integrating continuous innovation with established assurance and certification processes
- Human–machine interaction and user experience in vehicles  
- Open research challenges and roadmaps for automotive software engineering
- Industrial experience reports, case studies, and lessons learned

Submissions (6-12 pages) are to be made via the conference management system EasyChair using the following [link](https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=ase27). 

__Important dates__ (all AoE)

Submission deadline: October 21, 2026\
Notifications: November 25, 2026\
Final version: December 9, 2026

Submissions must be in English.

Furthermore, you must follow the [GI LNI Format Templates](https://github.com/gi-ev/LNI) (1-column A4 format). The programme committee will peer-review all submissions. Accepted submissions will be published in the SE 2027 workshop proceedings. For accepted submissions, at least one author is expected to register for the workshop. 

In case of queries or problems, please contact us by email: ase27@easychair.org
